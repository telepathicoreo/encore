Training rooms

Use Technique and Inspiration to sharpen a specific skill. Inspiration determines how much each session improves your stats.

Cost: 10 Technique + 5 Inspiration / session
Training costs 10 Technique and a fixed 5 Inspiration. Every point of Inspiration spent on training counts toward unlocking better practice facilities. Each facility specializes in different stats; upgrade when eligible to unlock a new set of training bonuses. Inspiration regenerates +5 every 5 minutes up to 1,000; boosters can reach 1,500.
